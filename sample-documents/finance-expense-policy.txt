Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy
Department: Finance
Effective date: 2026-01-01

1. Scope
This policy covers travel, meals, and other business expenses incurred by employees
while performing company duties.

2. Meal limits
Meal expenses are reimbursable up to $75 per person per day for domestic travel.
Alcohol is not reimbursable.

3. Receipts
Original itemized receipts are required for any expense over $25. Expenses without
receipts above that threshold will be denied.

4. Submission deadline
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Late
submissions require director approval.
