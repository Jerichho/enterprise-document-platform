Acceptable Use Policy
Department: Information Technology
Effective date: 2026-01-01

1. Company devices
Company-issued laptops and phones are for business use. Personal software may be
installed only from the approved IT catalog.

2. Data handling
Confidential customer and employee data must not be stored on personal cloud drives
such as personal Dropbox, Google Drive, or iCloud accounts.

3. Password requirements
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and rotated every 90 days. Multi-factor
authentication is required for all remote access.

4. Incident reporting
Suspected phishing or malware must be reported to security@example.com within
one business hour of discovery.
