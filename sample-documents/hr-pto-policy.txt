Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy
Department: Human Resources
Effective date: 2026-01-01

1. Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for paid time off after 90 days of employment.

2. Annual allotment
Employees receive twenty (20) days of paid time off each calendar year. Unused PTO
may be carried over up to a maximum of five (5) days into the following year.

3. Requests
PTO requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal,
except for unexpected illness or emergencies.

4. Blackout periods
PTO is generally not approved during the annual financial close window in the first
two weeks of January, unless approved by a department director.
